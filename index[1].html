How it works

A quick tour of everything you can do here.

Playing a round
  • Press Start all words. You see the meaning and hear the word.
  • Type the word and press Enter (or Check). After a correct answer, press Enter again or Next to move on.
  • Stop round pauses a round so you can resume it later, and the red cross beside the typing box removes the current word from the game.
  • Hear the word plays it again, First letter gives a hint, Example reads an example sentence aloud when a word has one, and Skip shows the answer.
  • After every 100 words you get a 1-minute break, with a warning during the last 10 words.
  • If you miss a word, retype it correctly. It comes back a few words later until you get it right.
  • American and British spellings both count.
Picking up later
  • Resume where I left off continues a round you did not finish, even after closing the page.
  • Practise my missed words appears after a round with misses. It replays only the words you missed in the last round with the same topic and letter.
Top bar
  • Font and Colour change how the page looks.
  • Dark mode / Light mode switches the brightness.
  • Sound turns the typing click, the happy chime and the sad buzz on or off.
  • Hovering any button draws a glowing outline in your chosen colour, and Transition at the top of this tour turns the sliding effect off.
  • Start screen takes you back to the welcome page, and Help opens this tour again.
  • The Review your notes card at the bottom of the main page opens the words you saved with the note button, with American and British pronunciation, spelling variants, meaning, synonyms, antonyms and an example.
  • Log in on the start screen keeps your scores, notes, saved rounds and word changes with your profile on this device. A PIN of at least 4 digits is required. Use Download my progress there to move to another device.
Score history
  • Every finished round is saved with its date, score, topic and letter. Open Missed on a round to see the words you missed, or All missed words for every round. Only the latest 3 rounds show, and Previous rounds opens the rest in a popup.
  • Clear history starts it over.
Add your own words
  • Type one word per line, then a dash, colon or comma and the meaning. To add an example sentence, put it after a | symbol. The meaning and example are optional.
  • Your words are saved in this browser only.
View all words
  • Use Category to see one topic and Alphabetical to see the words that start with a letter. On the start screen you can also choose a topic and a letter to practise only those words.
  • Search to check if a word is already added.
  • The red cross removes a word. Show removed words lets you Restore one, Delete forever, or Delete all.
Using the note button 📝
  • Where it is: beside the sound button for each word in View all words, and next to the typing box (beside the red cross) during a round.
  • How to use it: press it once to save the word to your notes. It turns yellow. Press it again to take the word out.
  • Reviewing: open Review your notes from the choice screen after Start, or from the card at the bottom of the main page.
  • What you see for each word: American and British pronunciation, spelling variants, the meaning, up to 3 synonyms, up to 2 antonyms and an example sentence.

Why it helps

  • Save tricky words in the middle of a round without stopping, then study them calmly later.
  • Hear both accents, which helps you understand British and American speakers in IELTS Listening.
  • Learn synonyms and antonyms, which makes your Writing and Speaking sound more varied.
  • See each word in a sentence, so you remember how it is used.
  • Your notes stay saved on your device, or with your profile if you log in.
